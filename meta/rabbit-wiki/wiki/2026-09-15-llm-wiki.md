# LLM Wiki (Karpathy pattern)

## Term/Concept

**LLM Wiki** — a pattern (popularized by Andrej Karpathy) for building a personal knowledge
base where an LLM incrementally builds and maintains a persistent, structured markdown wiki,
rather than re-deriving synthesis from raw documents at every query (as in typical RAG setups).

## Definition

Instead of retrieving relevant chunks from raw source documents at query time, the LLM
**compounds** knowledge into a durable wiki: when a new source is added, the LLM reads it,
extracts key information, and integrates it into existing wiki pages — updating entity/concept
pages, revising summaries, flagging contradictions with prior claims, and strengthening the
evolving synthesis. The wiki is the persistent artifact; the LLM is the maintainer; the human
directs sourcing and questions.

Three architectural layers:
- **Raw sources** — immutable curated documents (articles, papers, recordings, etc.); read-only
  source of truth.
- **The wiki** — LLM-owned markdown files (summaries, entity pages, concept pages, an index,
  a log) that are created and revised as new sources arrive.
- **The schema** — a configuration document (e.g. `CLAUDE.md`/`AGENTS.md`) describing wiki
  structure, conventions, and workflows for ingest/query/lint.

Three core operations: **Ingest** (add a source, integrate it into the wiki), **Query** (ask
questions, synthesize cited answers, optionally file good answers back into the wiki as new
pages), and **Lint** (health-check for contradictions, staleness, orphan pages, missing
cross-references, and data gaps).

Two navigation aids: `index.md` (content-oriented catalog of wiki pages, read first when
answering a query) and `log.md` (chronological, append-only record of ingests/queries/lint
passes, parseable via a consistent line-prefix convention).
